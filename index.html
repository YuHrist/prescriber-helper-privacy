Prescriber Helper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026
Summary: Prescriber Helper does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or user data. It makes no network requests. Everything it does happens locally in your browser.

What the extension does

Prescriber Helper highlights a configured list of keywords on the web page you are viewing and lists each match in a side panel so you can jump to it. Keyword highlighting is its only function.

Data we collect

None. The extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, or page content. It contains no analytics, tracking, or telemetry, and it makes no requests to any external server.

Information the extension handles locally

  • Your settings. The keywords, categories, and site list you configure are stored only on your own device using the browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) so they persist between sessions. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Administrator-supplied settings (optional). If your organisation deploys the extension via enterprise policy, it reads a configuration your administrator provides (chrome.storage.managed). This is supplied by your organisation and is not collected by us.
  • Page text. When you press "Scan", the extension reads the visible text of the current page in order to find and highlight your keywords. This happens entirely within your browser, the text is not stored or transmitted, and it is discarded when you navigate away or clear the highlights.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • storage — to save your settings on your device and read organisation-supplied settings.
  • scripting — to run the highlighting logic on the page you are viewing.
  • sidePanel — to display the results side panel.
  • Host access — to highlight keywords on the sites you (or your organisation) configure. The extension only reads page text locally to highlight it; nothing is sent off your device.

Third parties

The extension does not share data with any third party, because it does not collect or transmit any data.
